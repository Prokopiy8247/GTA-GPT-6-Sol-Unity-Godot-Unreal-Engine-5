# Harborline final development report

## Delivered in the existing project

Harborline is an original, missionless free-roam scene in the existing Unity 6000.6.0f1 URP project. The approximately 600 × 600 m coastal map is generated by editor code and is the enabled first build scene. It contains a road grid, six traffic route loops, water and piers, a park, runway and helipad, nine service points, a player with camera and arsenal, initial NPC/vehicle populations, lighting, weather, effects, audio hooks, and IMGUI HUD/admin tools. A Windows standalone build was produced at [Builds/Harborline/Harborline.exe](Builds/Harborline/Harborline.exe). There is no mission system.

The art direction uses geometric coastal architecture and vehicles with warm concrete, cobalt, teal, coral and cream colors. [UnityGTAGPT6Sol.blend](UnityGTAGPT6Sol.blend) is the saved master Blender source. Authoring and exports used the dedicated MCP connection for this project at port **9879**. The project contains **38 original FBX models** under [Assets/GTA/Generated/Models](Assets/GTA/Generated/Models); the complete inventory and Blender QA links are in [BLENDER_ASSETS.md](BLENDER_ASSETS.md).

## Vehicles and weapons

The **nine authored vehicle models** are Coral Runner civilian car, Police Interceptor, Dockside Pickup, Sprint GT, Delivery Van, Tide motorcycle, Lagoon motorboat, Seabird helicopter and Coast Skimmer propeller plane. They instantiate as **five gameplay classes**: car, motorcycle, boat, helicopter and plane. The scene has ten initial traffic vehicles, nine parked vehicles and five dedicated police/motorcycle/boat/aircraft placements. Runtime traffic spawning and the `F1` spawn menu add access. Vehicle code covers entry/exit, simple carjacking crime reports, land suspension/grip/braking, motorcycle balance, boat buoyancy, helicopter lift, plane thrust/lift, health and explosions. The clean smoke confirmed short movement for two traffic vehicles and a port van, though SportsCar 02 was stopped at the five-second check. A scripted sports car drive crossed one road line without damage; longer land routes, boating and flight handling remain untested. There are no bicycle or jet classes.

The arsenal defines **11 weapon slots**: Fists, Baton, Sidearm, Longshot, Vector SMG, Dock Shotgun, Civic Rifle, Scout Rifle, Surveyor sniper rifle, Grenade and Launcher. Four distinct Blender weapon models represent pistol, SMG, shotgun and carbine silhouettes; other slots share visuals or lack a dedicated model. The code includes magazines, reload, recoil/spread, hitscan, grenades/rockets, melee hits and weapon attachment data, but advanced attachment visuals and a full customization purchase flow are absent. One injected pistol shot consumed ammunition; manual aiming and hit quality remain untested.

The remainder of the 38 Blender exports include the static and rigged player, two civilian looks, police and tactical uniforms, nine buildings, and authored street/nature props such as lamps, signals, bus stop, bench, hydrant, container, trees, helipad and pier. The rigged player imports with two animation clips, but the current scene uses procedural motion on a segmented player.

## Police, world systems and controls

Crime reports can come from direct police observations, delayed civilian witnesses, gunshots, assaults and vehicle theft. Heat maps to wanted levels **0–5**. Officers follow the player or last known position; line-of-sight loss changes pursuit to a search state, after which heat can decay. Higher levels spawn tactical officers and emergency cruisers. Scripted level 3 and pursuit activation worked in smoke. Arrest, roadblocks, PIT/spike strips and police helicopter pursuit are not implemented; the full 0–5 chase loop is untested.

Other implemented but partly unverified systems include third-person movement and first-person toggle, crouch, simple cover/stealth states, surface swimming and breath, parachute descent, pedestrian panic, day/night lighting, clear/cloudy/rain/fog/storm settings, radar/full map, health/armor/cash, service purchases, JSON save/load, and procedural sound/effects. One isolated cash/ammo save round trip passed; broader persistence, UI and audio quality still need QA. Population distance limits exist; no active LOD groups, occlusion pass or measured recording FPS are available.

Quick controls: `WASD` move/drive, mouse orbit/aim, left click attack, right click aim, `Shift` sprint, `Space` jump/handbrake/climb, `Ctrl/C` crouch or descend, `F` enter/exit, `E` interact, `Q` cover, `Z` stealth, `P` parachute, `R` reload, `1`–`8`/wheel/`Tab` weapons, `V` camera, `M` map, `F1` sandbox lab, `F2` help, `Esc` pause. `H` sounds the horn, `L` toggles headlights, `J` toggles a police siren; plane pitch uses Up/Down or `I/K`. [DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md) has the context-sensitive control table and rebuild instructions.

## Known issues and remaining work

The clean virtual-input batch smoke exited with code 0 and no logged exception, and RenderTexture world screenshots are visible. Two earlier hidden non-batch launches intermittently stalled during graphics/input initialization. The clean run reported HUD/admin UI images as `UNAVAILABLE_BATCH`, so a visible-window startup and UI review is still needed. Scripted pedestrian/player input, traffic movement, pistol firing, save/load and one clean short car crossing passed. The `F1` panel was adjusted to fit within 720p and retain scrolling, and that change is included in the tested player build; its appearance still needs visual review. No manual keyboard/mouse session, stable long drive, swimming, flight, service transaction or sustained performance test has been recorded. Rich cover actions, stealth takedowns, traversal, ragdolls, full NPC traffic rules, arrest/search tactics, public transit, wildlife, clothing, phone, radio, interiors and optional activities remain incomplete or absent. The airstrip is small relative to plane handling. The feature matrix gives a more detailed account without treating model presence as proof of gameplay.
